How to type notes
S R G M P D N notes (any case). Raag decides komal / tivra.
space next matra (beat)
SRGM four notes in one matra, for taans
- hold the note for a matra
.N mandra (lower octave, dot below)
S' taar (upper octave, dot above)
Rb force komal  M# force tivra
D= force shuddha (e.g. shuddha Dha in Bhairav)
(N)S kan swar: grace note N before S
_ empty matra  | ignored, use it to keep your place
Bols: one syllable per matra, separated by spaces. - shows as ऽ in Devanagari.
"Starts on matra" lets a bandish begin mid-cycle, e.g. 9 or 13 in Teentaal.
